About

Mike Flinn

Motion designer and multimedia producer with 10+ years at Apple, spanning roles across Apple Support YouTube, AppleCare, AI/ML, and News. I've produced motion graphics for the Apple Support YouTube channel — reaching over 2 million subscribers — created visual communication assets for AppleCare support advisors, and contributed video production work across AI/ML and News teams.

I specialize in translating complex technical material into clear, intentional motion — whether that's a how-to for a global audience or an internal explainer for a team of engineers. I'm also studying Web & Digital Design at University of Maryland Global Campus, and increasingly interested in the space where motion and interactive design meet.
