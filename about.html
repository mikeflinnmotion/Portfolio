About

Mike Flinn

Motion designer and multimedia producer with 10+ years at Apple, working across support, AI/ML, and news. I specialize in translating complex technical material into clear, intentional motion — whether that's a how-to for millions of viewers or an internal explainer for a roomful of engineers.

Currently on rotation with Apple News as a Design Producer, where I lead production planning and cross-functional coordination for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS features. Previously produced video and motion content for Apple AIML and Apple Support YouTube.

I'm also studying Web & Digital Design at University of Maryland Global Campus, and increasingly interested in the space where motion and interactive design meet.
